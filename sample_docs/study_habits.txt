Study Habits for Exam Season

Good study habits matter more than long study hours. Short, focused sessions of about forty minutes followed by a ten minute break help the brain stay alert. Sleeping well before an exam improves memory more than staying up late to revise.

Active recall means testing yourself instead of re-reading notes. Close the book and write down everything you remember, then check what you missed. Spaced repetition spreads reviews over days and weeks, which strengthens long-term memory.

Plan the week in advance. List every subject, estimate how long each topic needs, and place the hardest subjects in the hours when you feel most awake. Keep your phone in another room while studying, and drink water regularly.
